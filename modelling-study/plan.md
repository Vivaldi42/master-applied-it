# Plan: The birthday paradox

Research question: What is the probability that at least two people in a group of n people share the same birthday?

1. Define assumptions: independent birthdays, 365 equally likely dates and no leap day.
2. Choose group sizes and the number of simulation trials.
3. Use Python and NumPy to simulate groups and detect shared birthdays.
4. Estimate match probabilities and Monte Carlo uncertainty.
5. Validate estimates against the exact formula for different group sizes.
6. Plot probability versus group size with matplotlib.
7. Interpret results and discuss model limitations.
8. Create a poster presenting the question, method, results and conclusions.
